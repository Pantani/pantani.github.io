# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

International technical recruiters, hiring managers, and engineering leaders evaluating Danilo Pantani for senior Go, backend, distributed systems, platform, infrastructure, developer tooling, and blockchain infrastructure roles.

## Product Purpose

Present a factual, evidence-backed professional profile that helps Danilo earn interviews for remote international roles. The visual portfolio serves human readers, while dedicated one-column resumes provide predictable ATS parsing and print-ready PDFs.

## Brand Personality

Technical, direct, and credible. Confident without exaggeration, specific without becoming protocol-only, and readable by both recruiters and engineers.

## Anti-references

Generic personal-brand marketing, inflated metrics, keyword stuffing, blockchain-only positioning, dense skill clouds, decorative resume templates, multi-column ATS documents, skill bars, and claims that cannot be reconciled with public evidence.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with Go, backend, and distributed systems; use blockchain as differentiated depth.
2. Prefer verified impact and ownership over responsibility lists or vanity metrics.
3. Keep the visual portfolio expressive and keep ATS documents linear and conventional.
4. Make every public claim consistent across source, rendered PDF, and linked evidence.
5. Optimize for fast scanning, plain language, and international hiring contexts.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic headings, selectable text, keyboard-accessible links, strong contrast, clear focus states, reduced-motion support, responsive layouts, and print output that remains legible in black and white. ATS resumes must preserve a single reading order without icons, sidebars, tables, or hidden text.
